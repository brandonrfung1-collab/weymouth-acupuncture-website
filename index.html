What We Offer

A Comprehensive Health Care System

Acupuncture and herbal medicine comprise a comprehensive health care system developed in China with a history of over 2,500 years.

Acupuncture

Treatment involves the insertion of very thin needles into specific points on the body. The needles are sterile and disposable, used once and then disposed of in a sharps container.

Acumassage & Acupressure

Hands-on techniques rooted in the same Chinese medical tradition. Acumassage may be used alongside acupuncture when indicated for your condition.

Herbal Medicine

Authentic Chinese herbal medicine, used in conjunction with treatment when indicated to support the balance of the whole body system.
